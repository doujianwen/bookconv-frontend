## 5. ❌ Filtered-out Noise (no time invested)

- **YouTube videos** (DR 99 "Como converter vídeo para MP3", etc.): video description slots; can't reliably plant a backlink.
- **In-article deep links to specific conversion URLs** (e.g. cloudconvert.com/mp4-converter appearing in a Contentful AVIF article): editorial one-time mention, hard to replicate.
- **Irrelevant topics**: Just Delete Me, Free English Grammar Checker, Piano Audio to MIDI, Furniture Shop Perth, etc. — off-topic anchor text.
- **Single social/forum mentions** (Reddit-type, single posts): small volume, mostly nofollow.
