# Case Study Template — 案例写作模板

## 用途

将证据库中的素材转化为可对外使用的案例描述。

---

## 模板结构

### Section 1：Problem（问题）

**格式**：1-2 句，描述客户/场景面临的挑战

**示例**：
> New SEO/GEO project with no established search visibility. Target: achieve measurable AI Search presence within 30 days of launch.

**纪律**：
- 不要虚构客户痛点
- 基于真实场景描述

---

### Section 2：Action（行动）

**格式**：3-5 个 bullet points，描述关键动作

**示例**：
> - Built User Intent System: 267 structured questions, 20 clusters, 131 active monitors
> - Published 109 pages across blog/guide/convert types (58 blog, 21 guide, 30 convert)
> - Tracked Bing AI performance via weekly exports (7 snapshots from Aug 28 to Sep 7)
> - Monitored GSC daily via API (2 pull snapshots covering Jul 26 to Sep 4)

**纪律**：
- 动词开头，具体动作
- 包含可验证的方法论（如 "weekly exports"）
- 不要列出所有工作，只列关键动作

---

### Section 3：Evidence（证据）

**格式**：1-3 条，指向具体证据 ID

**示例**：
> - [EV-01] Bing AI citations first appeared on 2026-08-08 (0 → 4)
> - [EV-02] Largest single-day jump: +109 citations on 2026-08-25
> - [SYSTEM-1] User Intent System: 267 questions, 131 monitors, 129 citations (three-state)

**纪律**：
- 用证据 ID 引用，方便追溯
- 不要展开解释数字来源

---

### Section 4：Result（结果）

**格式**：1-3 句，陈述可量化的变化

**示例**：
> Site achieved measurable Bing AI visibility within 13 days of launch (first non-zero on 2026-08-08). Single-page citation reached 519 (highest in dataset). Query-level citation share for targeted intent cluster doubled from 19.35% to 46.02%.

**纪律**：
- 只用 A 级和 B 级证据
- 不添加未记录的数字
- 包含时间维度

---

### Section 5：Caveat（限制）

**格式**：1-2 句，声明数据局限

**示例**：
> All snapshot comparisons are based on cumulative reports with unverified window lengths. Daily series (EV-01, EV-02) represents merged exports, not continuous monitoring. Citation counts reflect AI visibility, not website traffic.

**纪律**：
- 必须包含 caveat
- 不要弱化 caveat（即使数据有限也要如实声明）

---

## 完整示例（GEO 案例）

**Title**: Building Measurable AI Search Visibility for a New Ebook Converter

**Problem**
New site with zero established presence in both traditional search and AI-generated answers. Goal: prove that systematic content + intent-driven publishing can generate measurable AI visibility.

**Action**
- Launched BookConv.com with 109 pages (blog/guide/convert) targeting ebook conversion intent
- Built User Intent System (267 questions, 20 clusters, 131 monitors) to identify high-value queries
- Published content with weekly Bing AI export tracking across 7 snapshots
- Monitored GSC daily via API (2 pull snapshots)

**Evidence**
- [EV-01] First Bing AI citation on 2026-08-08 (site-wide: 0 → 4)
- [EV-02] Peak single-day jump +109 citations on 2026-08-25 (38 → 147)
- [EV-07] Query "transfer ebooks between devices" citation share: 19.35% → 46.02%
- [EV-09] Top page /blog/best-ebook-reader-apps: 47 → 519 citations
- [SYSTEM-1] Intent System: 267 questions, 129 three-state citations

**Result**
Site achieved measurable Bing AI visibility within 13 days of launch. Single-page citation growth of +472 (best-in-dataset). Targeted query share nearly doubled in 10-day snapshot window.

**Caveat**
Snapshot comparisons use cumulative reports with unverified window lengths. Daily series represents merged exports, not continuous monitoring. Citation ≠ traffic.

## 模板填写检查清单

- [ ] Problem 是否真实可证？
- [ ] Action 是否包含可验证的方法论？
- [ ] Evidence 是否引用证据 ID？
- [ ] Result 是否只用 A/B 级证据？
- [ ] Caveat 是否声明数据局限？
- [ ] 是否没有任何因果推断？
