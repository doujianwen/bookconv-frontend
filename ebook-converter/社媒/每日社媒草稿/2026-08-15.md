## r/SEO
**标题**：I automated my daily GSC pull with an AI-written script — and learned Google rewrites yesterday's numbers

**正文**：
Sharing because it changed how I read my own dashboards — and the catch was an AI automation, not Google.

**Site**: a small ebook converter, brand-new domain, launched late July. I pull search numbers every morning.

**What AI did**: wrote a script that pulls my Google Search Console stats daily and charts the ranked-keyword curve. Hands-off, fast. For weeks I just watched it.

**What AI missed (the human catch)**: I compared two pulls a day apart. The same day's keyword count was 204 in one, 229 in the next. Google had released low-traffic terms from a hidden "(other)" bucket it hides for privacy. So AI's daily snapshot was real but incomplete — Google back-fills small queries for days.

**Second trap**: one morning showed 0 searches. Looked like a crash. It wasn't — Search Console lags 2–3 days, the data was just late. The script doesn't know that; I had to.

**The rule I keep now**:
- Count ranked keywords from the query dimension, drop the (other) row.
- Count impressions/clicks from the date dimension (the true total), never by summing keywords (that under-reports ~2x).
- Read the trend, not a single day's snapshot.

Honest totals: 229 keywords, 1,107 impressions, average position 47.8, 0 clicks yet. The 0 is my real next problem — Google shows me, nobody clicks. Working on titles/CTR now.

Anyone else had an AI automation give you daily numbers that turned out to be estimates Google quietly corrected?

## r/SideProject
**标题**：I let AI automate my daily traffic report — and almost misread a flat line as a failure

**正文**：
Solo, AI writes most of my code. War story about trust in your own dashboards.

**What I'm building**: an ebook converter (15 formats, 26 pairs), ~3 weeks live, mostly AI-written.

**What AI did**: wrote a script that pulls my search stats every morning and emails me a chart. Super convenient — I stopped thinking about how the numbers are made.

**Where it bit me**: one day the pull showed 0 searches. My gut: "the site died again." Reality: Google Search Console lags 2–3 days; that day's data just hadn't generated. The script reported the lag as a zero.

**The bigger one**: I compared two pulls 24h apart and the same day's keyword count had grown from 204 to 229. Google releases low-traffic terms late from a hidden bucket. My "stall" was actually the threshold catching up.

**The human fix (no code change)**: I now read the 7-day trend, not today's number, and I cross-check the keyword sum against Google's real total. The automation is still great — I just stopped treating its output as ground truth.

**Takeaway for my own course**: AI is excellent at building the pipeline. You still own reading the output. Automate the pull, verify the movement.

Anyone else automated a report and then almost believed its worst-day reading?
