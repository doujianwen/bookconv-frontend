Solo, but AI writes most of my code. Honest edition of a week where AI helped AND hurt.

**What I'm building**: an ebook converter — turns one ebook format into another (15 formats, 26 pairs). ~3 weeks live.

**What AI did**: wired up hosting, shipped the converter, generated 87 content pages, automated my daily traffic stats. Fast and mostly right.

**The takeaway I'm keeping for my own course**: AI gives you speed; you pay with vigilance. "Deploy success" is a claim, not proof. Verify what's live is your latest work.

Anyone else had AI (or a tool) set up something that looked fine but quietly pointed at the wrong thing?

## r/SEO
**标题**：I let AI automate my GSC pull — and it under-reported my traffic 2x for two weeks

**正文**：
Sharing because it cost me two weeks of wrong conclusions — and the culprit was an AI-written script, not Google.

**Site**: a small ebook-converter, brand-new domain, launched late July. I pull search numbers every day.

**What AI did**: wrote a daily stats script that sums visits by keyword and charts them. Looked clean.

**What AI got wrong**: it summed by the query dimension and silently dropped Google's hidden "(other)" bucket — the small searches Google won't show individually. So when I added up by keyword, I lost a chunk of real traffic.

**The human catch**: I cross-checked the keyword-sum against the real total Google shows. They didn't match. One "busy" day I thought had **71 visits** actually had **150**. ~2x off.

**The rule now**:
- Count ranked keywords one way.
- Count visits/clicks from the true total, never by summing keywords.

After fixing it, my "stalled" curve turned real: **204 searches** (up from 162 in a day, +26%), best day **221 views** (+69%).

**The less-fun part**: 0 clicks through yet. I rewrote 3 titles 3 days ago, no change — that's just Google taking days to re-read, not a bad title.

Anyone else had an AI-written (or any) script quietly drop a data bucket? How do you double-check your numbers?

## r/webdev
**标题**：AI wired up my Vercel deploy — and pointed it at the wrong repo for 6 days

**正文**：
Posting so nobody repeats my Tuesday–Sunday.

I run a Next.js app on Vercel, built largely with AI assistance.

**What AI did**: set up the git connection / deploy source. Fast.

**The bug**: it connected Vercel to an OLD repo I'd abandoned — not the one I actually work in. Two separate projects. Every push built the dead one. The dashboard said "Deploy success" the whole time. Self-consistent, wrong source.

**Why I missed it for days**:
1. My checks only compared my machine ↔ my code storage. None compared storage ↔ live host.
2. I blamed an unrelated DB password error (also fun, separate story).
3. The old code worked for everything except one feature — no loud failure, just a quietly dead feature.
4. I trusted the green badge.

**The human fix (no code change)**: pointed the host at the right repo, forced a ship. Feature works end to end now.

**The takeaway**: after any AI setup, confirm production == your latest version — not just that the button turned green. AI ships; you verify.

How do you verify what's really running in production after an AI-assisted deploy?
