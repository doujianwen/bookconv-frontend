## 0. 结论先行

### 0.1 ChatGPT 方案的一处方向性错误

ChatGPT 的叙事是「新站 1 个月 → 300+ 关键词 → 持续增长 → 拿去卖」。
但实测数据是：

- Google 自然点击 **全周期 6 次**
- 8/22 起展示量断崖 **−88.0%**，持续 14 天零回升
- 新关键词获取通道 **8/22 同日关闭**（+15~17/天 → +1/天）

**任何一个欧美客户只要让你共享一次 GSC 屏幕，30 秒内就会看到断崖。**
到那时你说过的每一句话（包括真实的那部分）都会被打折。所以「增长型叙事」不是措辞优化问题，是**结构性不可售**。

### 0.2 但也有一处 ChatGPT 完全没看到的金矿

我把 Bing AI 的 31 个被引页面按类型拆开，再除以各类型的**已发布页面总数**（实测分母：guide 22、blog 58、convert 30），得到两张表。

**表 A｜引用占比（分子视角）**

| 页面类型 | 被引页数 | 引用次数 | 占比 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `/blog/*` 长文 | 16 | 780 | **59.4%** |
| `/guide/*` 长文 | 12 | 458 | **34.9%** |
| 首页 | 1 | 63 | 4.8% |
| `/convert/*` 程序化页 | 1 | 11 | **0.8%** |
| 其他 | 1 | 2 | 0.2% |

**表 B｜被引率（分母视角，这才是真正的判据）**

| 页面类型 | 已发布页数 | 被引页数 | **被引率** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `/guide/*` | 22 | 12 | **54.5%** |
| `/blog/*` | 58 | 16 | **27.6%** |
| `/convert/*` | 30 | 1 | **3.3%** |

> 🔴 **修正说明：此前记忆库记录的「CONTENT_MAP ≈ 87+ 转换页」有误，实测 `src/data/content/index.ts` 仅 30 个转换对。** 分母修正后结论反而更强。

**关键数字：guide 页被引率 54.5%，程序化页 3.3% —— 相差 16.5 倍。**
30 个程序化页只贡献 0.8% 的引用；28 篇长文贡献 94.3%。

而 Google 侧断崖里，被收回展示的恰恰是这些内页（`/convert/*` 保留率 8.2%、`/blog/*` 3.3%），首页保留率 79.6%。
这与记忆库 §9/4 的独立发现完全一致：**暗页率 convert 90% / blog 72% / guide 20%**——guide 既是被引率最高的，也是暗页率最低的。两条独立测量互相印证。

**⇒ 两条数据线互相咬合，指向同一个结论：**

> **Long-form content earns AI citations. Programmatic pages don't — and they are exactly what the spam update took away.**

这才是你能拿去卖的东西。它不是「我让站点涨了」（海外一抓一把），而是一个**有分母、有对照、可证伪的内容形态判据**。

## 2. 核心方法论：三条可执行判据

这一节是你真正卖的产品。每条都配了实测反例。

### 判据一｜内容形态决定 AI 引用，不是页面数量

- guide 页被引率 **54.5%**（12/22），blog **27.6%**（16/58），程序化页 **3.3%**（1/30）
- 30 个程序化页 → 0.8% 的引用；28 篇长文 → 94.3%
- **交叉印证**：暗页率 convert 90% / blog 72% / guide 20%（独立测量，卡方显著）
- **推论**：在 AI 搜索时代，用程序化页堆覆盖面的 ROI 接近 0；**每页被引概率差 16.5 倍**
- ⚠️ **置信度说明**：这是相关性，不是因果。guide 页主题本身更偏「How/Why 型」，与 AI 查询意图天然更契合；存在主题选择偏差的可能，未做对照实验

### 判据二｜程序化规模化内容是 spam update 的主要打击面

- 8/18 Google spam update 后，内页保留率 3.3%–8.8%，首页 79.6%
- 双峰分布极其干净：首页型泛词活着甚至涨（`epub para doc` 保留 132%），格式转换词基本全灭（`mobi to epub` 0%、`lit to epub` 0%、`azw3 vs mobi` 0%）
- **推论**：规模化内容与算法风险的权衡，需要在架构阶段就做，不是事后补救

### 判据三｜单一查询可以拿到压倒性引用份额

- `Harry Potter digital books multiple devices` → **57.78% citation share**
- `convert mobi to epub` → **25.62%**
- **推论**：长尾 + 具体实体 + 多设备/场景约束的组合，是 AI 引用的高杠杆点

## 3. 定位（用户选定：全渠道 AI Search Visibility）

### 3.1 头衔

```
AI Search Visibility Specialist
— measuring and improving how often your content gets cited
  in Google AI and Copilot answers
```

副标题（视场合二选一）：
- `Built and measured it on my own site first.`
- `I publish my failures too.`

### 3.2 一句话定位

> **I help B2B and publisher sites get cited in AI answers — and I show you the measurement, not a screenshot.**

### 3.3 为什么不用 "GEO Freelancer"

- GEO 在欧美已被用滥，且大量从业者拿不出测量口径
- 「Specialist」+ 明确的 measurement 承诺，可直接把 90% 的竞品排除
- 你的差异化不是工具，是**你会审计自己**（三轮苏格拉底审计推翻过自己两个结论，有文档为证）

---

### 3.4 ⚠️ 「全渠道」定位的硬伤与三种解法

**问题**：你选了「全渠道 AI Search Visibility」，但实测 Google 生成式 AI 只有 **16 次展示**（5 个页面），且自 8/21 起连续 13 天归零。
一个美国客户问「你在 Google AI 上的成果呢」，你拿不出东西。

| 解法 | 做法 | 优点 | 风险 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. 主动披露 + 重新框定**（推荐） | 对外明说「我的站 Bing AI 成立、Google AI 不成立，两个都能给你看」 | 可信度最高，把短板变成「只有我才告诉你」；与已选定的双轨叙事同构 | 需要客户能理解「诊断能力 > 单渠道成果」 |
| B. 改名 Bing/Copilot 细分 | 定位收缩到 Bing & Copilot AI Visibility | 全部证据都成立，无短板 | 客户池小；放弃了你已选的全渠道 |
| C. 只讲 Bing，回避 Google | 材料里不提 Google AI | 短期看起来最漂亮 | 🔴 **客户一问就穿帮，且违反诚实铁律。不采用** |

**推荐 A 的具体做法**：把「两个渠道都测」本身作为产品卖点。

> "I measure both channels on my own site, and the results are lopsided: Bing and Copilot cite me, Google's AI surfaces essentially don't — 16 impressions in six weeks. Most people would show you only the one that worked. I'll show you both, because when I audit your site, you're going to get the same honesty about which channel is actually moving."

**为什么这反而更强**：你的差异化本来就不是「成果漂亮」，而是「测量口径可信 + 敢报失败的那一半」。
Google AI 归零这一点，恰好是这条差异化最锋利的证明。

⚠️ **但有一个前提**：如果客户的核心诉求就是「我要 Google AI 流量」，那 A 也救不了，应诚实说「这不是我的强项，我可以帮你测，但我不承诺结果」。

## 4. 双轨叙事结构（用户选定：AI 成果 + Google 复盘）

### 4.1 叙事骨架

**Track A（成果）**：Bing/Copilot AI 侧
- 41 天，1,314 次 AI 引用，31 个被引页面
- 单日峰值 198，峰值被引页 22
- 单查询 citation share 最高 57.78%
- **被引率按类型分化：guide 54.5% / blog 27.6% / 程序化页 3.3%** → 判据一

**Track B（复盘）**：Google 侧
- 8/18 spam update，8/22 展示 −88.0%，新词通道同日关闭
- 归因过程：imp/query 恒定 → 排除需求变化；首页豁免 79.6% vs 内页 3.3–8.8% → 双峰 → 定位到规模化内容
- 稳定期纪律：14 天不 panic-edit、不删页、不合并
- 独立印证：暗页率 convert 90% / blog 72% / guide 20% → 判据二

**合流点**：两条线指向同一条判据 —— 长文拿 AI 引用，程序化页不拿且被打。
**⇒ 这不是两个故事，是一个故事的两半。**

### 4.2 三个时长的话术版本

**30 秒版（电梯/私信开场）**

> I run an ebook converter site as a live lab. In 41 days it earned 1,300+ AI citations across 31 pages in Bing and Copilot — one query hit 57% citation share. Here's the finding that matters: my long-form guide pages got cited on 54.5% of them, my blog posts 27.6%, and my 30 programmatic conversion pages — 3.3%. In the same window Google cut my impressions 88% after the August spam update, and it took the programmatic pages. Two channels, one conclusion. That's what I help companies audit and fix.

**3 分钟版（首次通话）**
按 4.1 骨架展开，每段结尾抛一个反例，主动暴露弱点。

**15 分钟版（pitch deck）**
结构：
1. 我做了什么（站点 + 内容架构）
2. 发生了什么（双渠道数据卡）
3. 我怎么归因的（三个判据 + 各自反例）
4. 我踩了什么坑（断崖、程序化页）
5. 我怎么给你做（服务包）
6. 你怎么验证我（数据口径全公开）

### 4.3 三条不可逾越的话术红线

| ❌ 不许说 | ✅ 应该说 |
|---|---|
| "AI 推荐了我的站 150–225 次/天" | "measured 1,300+ AI citations in 41 days" |
| "我的 GEO 方法让 AI 引用我" | "here's what I built, here's what happened, here's the data" |
| "新站 1 个月做到 X 增长" | "41 天，双渠道结果分化，我做了归因" |
| "能给你带来流量" | "能提高被 AI 引用的概率与可测量性" |

## 5. 服务包设计（用户选定：月度 retainer $800–1,500）

### 5.1 ⚠️ 一个必须先说清的风险

**Retainer 是三种形态里最难冷启动的**：客户要连续付费 3 个月才认账，而你现在**零付费客户、零第三方背书**。

我的建议（不改变你的选择，只是加一道门）：
**在 retainer 前加一个 $600 一次性诊断作为入口，可全额抵扣首月 retainer。**

理由：
- 客户决策门槛从「$1,200/月 × 3 个月 = $3,600」降到「$600 试一次」

## 6. 客户会问的尖锐问题（英文话术）

**Q1. "Your site only got 6 clicks from Google. Why should I hire you?"**

> Correct — 6 clicks from Google, 13 from Bing, 19 total over 41 days. This is a visibility case study, not a traffic case study, and I say that up front. What I'm selling is measurement and diagnosis, not a promise of clicks. If you want someone to promise you traffic, I'm not your guy. If you want to know exactly how often AI answers cite you, which pages, on which queries, and why the rest are invisible — that's what I do.

**Q2. "Is your site penalized?"**

> It took an 88% impression drop on August 22, consistent with the August 18 spam update. I attribute it to scaled content — that's inference, not an official Google statement, and I'll tell you my confidence level rather than pretend it's certain. What's relevant for you: I diagnosed it in 72 hours, I did not panic-delete or mass-merge pages, and I built a re-test criterion with a defined date. That discipline is what you're buying.

**Q3. "Can you prove your method caused the AI citations?"**

> No, and I won't claim it. The step on August 25 from 38 to 147 citations has no action of mine that fully explains it. What I can prove is the structural finding, measured with real denominators: 54.5% of my guide pages got cited, 27.6% of my blog posts, 3.3% of my 30 programmatic pages — a 16.5x spread. That's a correlation with a clean counter-example, not a causal claim. And I'll name the weakness myself: guide topics skew toward how-and-why questions, which may naturally match AI query intent. I haven't run a controlled test on that, and I'd rather tell you than let you find out.

**Q4. "Bing has ~4% market share. Why does this matter?"**

> Two reasons. First, Copilot is embedded in Windows and Microsoft 365 — your buyers use it whether or not they "use Bing." Second, the measurement infrastructure exists on the Bing side today: per-query citation counts, cited pages, citation share. Google's generative AI report only went global on August 31. If you want to be early on AI visibility measurement, Bing is where the data is right now, and I read both.

**Q5. "How is this different from my current SEO agency?"**

> Most agencies report rankings and sessions. Neither tells you whether an AI answer is citing you when your buyer asks a question. I measure citation frequency, cited pages, and citation share per query, and I report every month in the same format so you can see movement. Also — I publish my failures. If a conclusion I gave you last month doesn't hold, you'll read about it in next month's report.

**Q6. "What if I don't see results after 3 months?"**

> Then you'll have 3 months of clean baseline data and a documented list of what doesn't work on your site — which is more than you have now, and it's yours to keep. I don't lock you into reporting you can't take elsewhere. That said, I'd rather run the $600 diagnostic first and tell you honestly whether there's anything worth fixing.

**Q7. "Can't I just do this myself / with a tool?"**

> You can pull the data. Most tools stop there. The part that took me 40 days to build is the diagnosis layer: separating page-type effects from content effects, filtering dirty rows before comparing sets, and defining what would falsify my own conclusion. I've overturned two of my own findings in three audit rounds, and both are documented.

**Q8. "Where are you based? Why should I trust you?"**

> I'm based in China; I work async with US and EU clients and all reporting is in English with timestamps in your timezone. On trust: everything I claim is measured in tools you own — your Search Console, your Bing Webmaster Tools. I never show you a screenshot I can't reproduce with your credentials.

**Q9. "How visible are you in Google's AI results?"**

> Sixteen impressions in six weeks, across five pages, and zero since August 21. That's the honest number. Bing and Copilot cite me constantly — 1,314 times — Google's AI surfaces essentially don't, on the same site in the same weeks. I'd rather tell you that than let you find it. What it means for you: if your buyers search on Google AI surfaces, I'll measure it for you honestly, but I won't promise you a result I haven't produced on my own site. What I can promise is that you'll know your baseline in both channels before you spend anything.

| 论点 | 类型 | 置信度 | 依据 / 反例 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bing AI 累计 1,314+ 次引用、31 页 | **实测** | 高 | WMT 导出，两表交叉 |
| 94.3% 引用来自 blog+guide 长文 | **实测** | 高 | 31 页全量，非抽样 |

| Google −88.0% 断崖 | **实测** | 高 | GSC API 逐日 |
| 断崖归因 = 8/18 spam update 命中规模化内容 | **推断** | **中高** | imp/query 恒定 + 首页豁免双峰 + 官方确认该更新；⚠️ 无 Google 官方点名本站 |
| 8/25 AI 引用阶跃原因 | **未知** | — | 🔴 无任何动作可解释，**禁止对外声称因果** |
| Google 生成式 AI 仅 16 次展示 | **实测** | 高 | 四向交叉校验（图表=网页=国家=设备=16）；UI 人工导出，API 不支持 |
| Google AI 归零早于自然断崖 1 天 | **观察** | **低** | n=16，日级模式在噪声水平；只能稳健地说「自 8/21 起连续 13 天零」 |
| 西语页 `/es/blog/azw3-vs-mobi` 占 AI 展示 62.5% | **实测** | 中 | n=16 分母过小，不足以外推；但值得列为待查问题 |
| Bing AI 是否进入衰减 | **未定论** | 低 | 判据：9/02、9/03 连续 ≤117 才算衰减 |
| 全站自然点击 19 次 | **实测** | 高 | Google 6 + Bing 13 |
| 「长文→AI 引用」是因果还是相关 | **相关** | **中** | 有分母、有对照（16.5× 差距），但⚠️ **存在主题选择偏差**：guide 主题偏 How/Why，天然更贴 AI 查询意图；未做对照实验，对外必须主动声明 |

### 8.1 三个必须记住的纪律

1. **永远用保守数**（1,314 而非 1,530），并在材料里注明口径。
2. **每个结论配反例**。你自己的记忆库里写着「小样本强结论已两次犯错」——对外材料同理。
3. **v1 冻结后不改**。数据更新另起 v2 文件，as-of date 必须写。
