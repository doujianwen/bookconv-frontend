Frequently Asked Questions

Is BookConv free to use?

Yes! BookConv is completely free to use with no registration required. Uploads are limited to 100 MB per file.

What formats does BookConv support?

BookConv supports 28+ formats including EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, DOCX, TXT, RTF, HTML, FB2, LIT, CBR, DJVU, JPG, PNG, and Word documents.

Is my data safe when I upload files?

Absolutely. All files are transferred over encrypted HTTPS connections and automatically deleted after the conversion completes. We never read, store, or share your content.

Do I need to install any software?

No! BookConv runs on our servers using the same Calibre engine as the desktop app. Upload your file, pick the output format, and download the result — no software installation needed. Your file is encrypted in transit and automatically deleted after the conversion completes.

Can I convert Kindle books to EPUB?

Yes! BookConv supports converting AZW3 and MOBI (Kindle formats) to EPUB and other formats. Simply upload your Kindle file and choose your desired output format.
